Transcribe iPhone Voice Memos on your Mac

Record on your iPhone, and find the transcript waiting on your Mac. Free, offline, and nothing is uploaded.

Before you start

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later, with Matita installed.
  • Voice Memos syncing through iCloud. On your iPhone: Settings → your name → iCloud → turn on Voice Memos. Do the same on your Mac, then open the Voice Memos app on the Mac once so your recordings come across.

Steps

  1. Click “From your iPhone” on Matita's main window.
  2. Give Matita Full Disk Access. macOS keeps Voice Memos private, so the first time Matita shows you how: open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access and turn on Matita (or add it with +). Then quit Matita (⌘Q) and open it again. Matita only reads your recordings; it never changes or uploads them.
  3. Pick the memos you want transcribed, or search them by title.
  4. Turn on “Transcribe new memos automatically” if you want every new memo done for you. Matita checks for new ones every couple of minutes, using the language and model on the main window.

A memo is greyed out? It's still in iCloud. Play it once in the Voice Memos app on your Mac to download it.

Good settings for voice memos

  • Language: choose yours, or Detect automatically if you mix languages between memos.
  • Model: Turbo for the best accuracy. Parakeet is fastest for English, French, Spanish and German.
  • Titles: with AI assist on, each transcript comes with title ideas, so “New Recording 23” can get a name you'll find later.

Questions

Does Matita upload my voice memos?

No. It reads them from your Mac's Voice Memos library and transcribes them on your Mac.

Why does Matita need Full Disk Access?

macOS stores Voice Memos in a protected folder. Full Disk Access is the only way for another app to read it.
