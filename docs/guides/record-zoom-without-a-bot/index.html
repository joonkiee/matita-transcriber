Record and transcribe a Zoom call without a bot

Matita records your microphone and the other people in the call straight from your Mac. Nobody new joins the meeting.

Ask first. Tell the people in the call that you're recording. In many places recording a conversation without consent is against the law.

Before you start

  • macOS 14.2 or later. Recording the sound of other apps needs it.
  • On Matita's main window, set Speakers to the number of people in the call (2 people, 3 people…). It separates voices better than Auto.

Steps

  1. Click Record just before the meeting starts.
  2. Turn on “Include sound from other apps”. This records the other people too. The first time, macOS asks you to allow Matita to use the microphone and to record audio from other apps.
  3. Keep the recording window open. Your Mac won't go to sleep while recording. Use Pause if you need a break.
  4. Click “Stop and transcribe” when the call ends.
  5. Open the transcript. Name the speakers by clicking “Speaker 1” and so on, and use the Speakers tab to read what each person said.

Afterwards

  • With AI assist on, you get an overview, action items and title ideas.
  • Save it as Word or Markdown, or send it to Obsidian.

Questions

Does this work with Teams and Google Meet?

Yes. Matita records the sound your Mac plays, so it works with any call app, including in a browser.

Does anyone see that I'm recording?

No bot joins, so the call app doesn't announce it. That's why you should tell people yourself.
