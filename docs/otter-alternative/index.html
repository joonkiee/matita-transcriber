Matita

A free, offline Otter alternative for Mac

Matita transcribes meetings, lectures and the recordings you already have, on your own Mac. No minute limits, no upload, no account.

Otter is a cloud service: your audio goes to Otter's servers and your transcripts live in Otter's app. Matita does the same job on your Mac. It's free and open source, and every transcript is a plain file in a folder you choose.

When Matita is the better fit

  • You'd rather your audio didn't leave your Mac. Speech recognition runs on your Mac with whisper.cpp or NVIDIA Parakeet. Summaries can run on your Mac too, with Ollama.
  • You transcribe a lot. There are no monthly minutes and no length limit per recording.
  • You have recordings already. Drop in any audio or video file, or let Matita watch a folder.
  • You keep notes in files. Transcripts are .txt and .srt files; you can save Word or Markdown, or write notes straight into Obsidian.

When Otter is the better fit

  • You need it on Windows, Android or in a browser. Matita runs only on Macs with Apple Silicon.
  • You want transcripts shared with your team in one online workspace.
  • You'd like a bot that joins your calendar's meetings for you.

Getting started

  1. Download Matita and drag it into Applications.
  2. Open it once. Matita isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer ID, so macOS asks you to confirm: click Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  3. Add a recording (or press ⌘O), or click Record. The first time, Matita downloads a speech model (640 MB to 1.6 GB).

Questions

Is Matita really free?

Yes. Matita is open source under the MIT licence. There's no paid plan, no account and no usage limit.

Does Matita need an internet connection?

Only to download a speech model the first time, to check for updates, and, if you choose Claude for summaries, to send the transcript to Anthropic. Transcription itself works offline.

Can Matita record Zoom, Teams or Google Meet?

Yes, on macOS 14.2 or later. Turn on “Include sound from other apps” when recording, and Matita records your microphone and the other people in the call. No bot joins.
